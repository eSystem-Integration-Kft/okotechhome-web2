Cookie notice

Some cookies are needed for the site to work; others need your consent. Here we set out, category by category, which is which.

Introduction

What cookies are, and why we use them

Last updated: 7 August 2026.

A cookie is a small data file the website places on your device and the browser sends back on later visits. Some are needed for the site to work, some serve convenience, and some are for measurement or advertising.

Cookies strictly necessary for operation need no consent, but we must inform you about them. Every other cookie requires prior consent , which you may withdraw at any time, as easily as you gave it.

Cookie categories

Which cookies we use

You can change the setting for each category at any time in the cookie settings.

Settings

How to change your consent

On the website

In the cookie settings you can allow or block non-essential cookies by category. Withdrawal is as simple as giving consent, and it does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

MISSING DATA: the cookie consent interface (banner and preference manager) is not yet on the site. Until it is, cookies requiring consent cannot lawfully be set. To be supplied before go-live.

In the browser

Every common browser lets you view, delete and block cookies. The setting is usually found under “Privacy” or “Settings”. If you block all cookies, some functions of the site will not work.

Recording consent

We record your consent and its time so that it can be demonstrated. This is itself processing necessary for operation, on the legal basis of complying with our legal obligations.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

What happens if I do not accept cookies?

The site will work. Strictly necessary cookies are set regardless — they need no consent — but the others are not. The only visible consequence is that the embedded map will not load and we will not measure your visit.

Do you track me for advertising?

We currently use no advertising or remarketing cookies. If that changes, this notice will be updated and we will ask for consent as a separate category.

How long is my consent valid?

We keep the cookie setting for up to 12 months, after which we ask again. You may of course change it sooner at any time.
