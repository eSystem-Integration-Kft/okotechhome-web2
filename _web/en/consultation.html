Request a consultation

A few questions about the property and your situation — after which you can ask for a discussion by phone, online or on site, at times that suit you. Your answers form a technical summary, so that the first conversation is already about what matters.

Why we ask so much

A good recommendation needs data, not guesswork

The size and the type are decided together by the property, the load and the plot. Leave blank anything you do not know — we fill the gaps at the consultation.

  • 5–7 perc

  • Only the questions that matter

    The pages follow your earlier answers — we do not ask about industrial load for a holiday home, or company details from a private individual.

  • A reply within two working days

    We confirm one of the times you give, or propose another if none of them fits the calendar.
