I already have a system and need help

If the system is already running, the question is not choosing but operating. The entry points below lead to the Customer support section: faults, maintenance, parts and service. You can come to us with a unit that did not come from us — we will say so if we cannot help.

Support

What can we help with?

  • Identifying a fault

    Odour, backing up, an unusual noise or a stopped compressor: the first step is to decide whether the cause is operational or technical.

    Faults→

  • Routine maintenance

    Checking the sludge level, inspecting the membrane and the compressor, and periodic sludge removal — these are what give the system its life.

    Maintenance→

  • Parts and replacement

    Compressor, membrane, cover and other wearing parts. The exact type can be read from the unit's rating plate.

    Parts→

  • Assessing an existing system

    If you do not know what system is on the plot, or it has not been inspected for a long time: the survey shows its condition and what has to be done next.

    Request a survey→

First steps

What is worth checking before you call a service engineer

In some cases the problem has an operational cause and can be settled with a few minutes' checking. This list does not replace a service call, but it helps describe the situation more precisely.

  • 01

    Is there power at the unit? A tripped circuit breaker or an unplugged connector is the most common reason a compressor has stopped.

  • 02

    Is the compressor running? A quiet, even sound is normal. Complete silence and unusually loud, uneven running are both signals.

  • 03

    When was the sludge last removed? Sludge removal left undone is the most common cause of falling efficiency and of odour.

  • 04

    Has the load changed? More occupants, a longer stay by guests, or several weeks away all affect how the system behaves.

  • 05

    Has anything gone into the system that does not belong there? Wet wipes, fat, paint, medication or disinfectant in large quantities all harm the biological process.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

The unit did not come from you — can you help?

In many cases yes, particularly on operating questions and for a survey. With parts the type decides: if that manufacturer's component cannot be obtained, we say so rather than recommending an unsuitable substitute.

How often does the sludge have to be removed?

That depends on the load and the type of unit, so it cannot be stated in general. Checking the sludge level is part of routine maintenance, and the timing of removal follows from it — planning by the calendar is not the way.

What does it mean if I notice a smell near the system?

Odour is always a signal, not a normal operating state. Its most common causes are sludge removal left undone, a loss of aeration, or a blocked vent. If the power supply and the compressor are in order, it is worth calling a service engineer.
