I am looking for a solution for a business or institution

„How many people use it?” is particularly misleading here. A guesthouse, a school and a plant may all give the same number — and produce three entirely different projects.

The opening question

„How many people?” is particularly misleading here

For business or institutional wastewater, „how many people use it?” is not enough information to size on. The daily volume of water, the peak load, how occupancy varies over time, and the wastewater itself origin matters at least as much.

A twenty-bed guesthouse, a three-hundred-pupil school and a plant employing fifty may all give the same number — and produce three entirely different projects.

So this page does not recommend a product as its first step. Its purpose is to categorise it: whether it can be treated as a standard domestic-type load, or whether it calls for bespoke engineering, pre-treatment, laboratory work, permitting and operational planning.

The first separation

What is the origin of the wastewater?

Domestic in character

  • Bathroom, WC, handbasin, washing
  • Its composition resembles household wastewater
  • The load is proportional to the number of users
  • Conventional biological treatment can be applied
  • Laboratory analysis is typically not a precondition
  • Most accommodation, offices and schools belong here

Process or commercial kitchen

  • Arising from a production process or a production kitchen
  • Its composition depends on the activity
  • It cannot be derived from headcount
  • It may require pre-treatment
  • Laboratory analysis is typically unavoidable
  • Industry-specific discharge requirements may apply

Which kind of facility?

Pick the closest one

The logic of the load differs by type of facility — which is why we treat them separately rather than as a single „B2B” category.

  • Guesthouses and accommodation

    Bed count is not load. Occupancy, peak weekends, season, laundry, spa — and operation out of season.

    Guesthouse, accommodation→

  • Restaurants and commercial kitchens

    Kitchen wastewater cannot be treated like that of a dwelling with the same headcount. Pre-treatment and laboratory data.

    Restaurant, kitchen→

  • Schools and institutions

    A strong daily rhythm, a drop at weekends, long holidays — and an organisational question over who the responsible operator will be.

    School, institution→

  • Campsites and community facilities

    Wildly varying load, several points of use and shared operating responsibility.

    Campsite, community→

  • Plants and special loads

    „Plant” is not a sizing category. Separating the domestic and process water streams, laboratory data.

    Plant, industrial→

  • Technical project brief

    A structured data package from which it can be decided whether a standard configuration or engineering design follows.

    Project brief→

What has to be settled on every project

Seven questions, in order

Together these answers establish whether this is a standard or a bespoke project.

  • 01

    What is the facility, and what is the origin of the wastewater? This is the single most important question — everything else follows from it.

  • 02

    What is the actual load? Average daily volume of water, peak, seasonality. At an existing facility the water bill is the best starting point.

  • 03

    Is there any special water use? A kitchen, a laundry, a spa, process washing — each is a separate water stream.

  • 04

    Which capacity category does it fall into? The legal boundary is 50 PE — above it a different design and permitting route follows.

  • 05

    Where does the treated water go? Into the ground, to a surface receiving water, or for reuse — all three are separate routes.

  • 06

    Who will be the responsible operator? On a business project this is an organisational question, not only a technical one.

  • 07

    What expansion is expected? If the facility grows, that has to be allowed for now.

What this a hub not tesz

It gives no price and promises no permit. On a business or institutional project, a price quoted from capacity or bed count would be particularly misleading — the whole project and life-cycle cost is determined together by the system architecture, the pre-treatment, the installation, the monitoring and the operation.

Nor does it substitute for the engineering decision. Where the wastewater is not domestic in character, laboratory data and a feasibility study come before the quote — not the other way round.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Is it enough if I say how many of us work there?

Fine as an entry figure, but not sufficient. For a business or institutional load, headcount is particularly misleading: what counts is how much water is used, when, and whether the wastewater is domestic in character. At an existing facility the water bill is the best starting point.

Do you treat abattoir or dairy wastewater?

We handle special loads on the basis of bespoke engineering analysis. This is not automatic suitability: sampling, laboratory data and a feasibility study come first. A standard plant is not automatically suitable for any industrial wastewater — we say so in advance.

How long until I get a quote?

It depends on the data available. For a purely domestic load with known water consumption, quickly. For process wastewater, laboratory analysis and a feasibility study come first — there the first step is sampling, not the quote.

Is a permit required?

On a business or institutional project typically yes, and the procedure depends on the capacity, the origin of the wastewater and the receiving water. The permitting questions are covered in detail on the larger-systems page in the Solutions section.
