Technical project brief

Not a general quotation request. The aim is that our specialist receives a structured decision situation — and that you are not told back that „this needs more data”.

More than a request for a quote

An institution's or a business's wastewater load cannot be described with a few headcount and plot fields. So this page is not a general quotation request but a structured project data package.

From it, it can be decided whether the project goes towards a standard configuration, detailed engineering sizing, an on-site survey, laboratory analysis or a separate feasibility study.

The aim is that our specialist receives a structured decision situation rather than a raw enquiry of the „price for a 200-bed hotel please” kind — and that you are not told back that „this needs more data”.

What the brief records

Six groups of data

  • 01

    Project identification. Type of facility, municipality, project stage, intended commissioning date, new or existing system.

  • 02

    Load. Nominal headcount or bed count, average daily occupancy, peak occupancy, average and maximum m³/day, operating days, seasonal curve, shifts.

  • 03

    The character of the wastewater. Purely domestic, commercial kitchen, process, or a mixture of several origins; existing laboratory results; existing pre-treatment.

  • 04

    Plot and receiving water. Available area, pipe levels, soil and groundwater, receiving water, mains sewer, existing network.

  • 05

    Technical readiness. Existing drawings, process water streams, headroom for expansion, monitoring requirements, redundancy requirements.

  • 06

    Operation. Responsible operator, maintenance model, service expectations, documentation, record-keeping, procurement process.

The output

What it returns, and what it does not

What you get

  • Project category — which route it falls into
  • Completeness of the data: what is in place, what is missing
  • The laboratory or survey work required
  • Which expert it is worth involving
  • An agenda for the consultation
  • State of readiness for a quote

Ezt not kapja

  • An automatic specific price
  • A price band for an unsettled project scope
  • A guaranteed capacity from incomplete data
  • Automatic industrial sizing without laboratory data
  • A promise that the project can be permitted
  • A final product model

What is worth attaching

Documents

With these we reach a technical direction considerably faster. We use your data solely to answer your enquiry.

  • 01

    Site plan

  • 02

    Mechanical drawings

  • 03

    A consumption series — water bills or meter readings

  • 04

    Laboratory results, where available

  • 05

    An earlier drawing or permit

  • 06

    An existing quote, if you would like a comparison

For industrial wastewater

One separate rule

For process wastewater, without laboratory results or representative sampling the brief not ad a recommended product or size. The result is then „expert examination required” — and that is not a refusal but the correct first step.

This matters because on an industrial project the most expensive mistake is not the wrong unit but a discharge requirement that cannot be met. That is considerably harder to correct afterwards.

In the meantime

Let us work through it together

Until the structured brief is running, we go through the same points in person. Write and tell us the type of facility, the scale, the operating profile and where the project stands — we will ask about the rest.

If you have documents — a site plan, a water bill, laboratory results, an earlier drawing — attach them: with those we reach a technical direction far more quickly.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Why do I not get a price straight away?

Because on a business or institutional project a price quoted from capacity or bed count would mislead. The whole project and life-cycle cost is determined together by the system architecture, the pre-treatment, the installation, the monitoring and the operation. With a settled project scope, however, a price band can be given.

How much data does an enquiry need?

For a conversation, the type of facility and the scale are enough. To name a technical direction we need the load profile and the character of the wastewater. For a quote, the plot, the receiving water and the operating model on top of those.

We are in a procurement process and need a technical specification.

We are glad to help with that. Precise technical content matters precisely because the comparability of the bids turns on it — and an incomplete specification produces bids that cannot be compared.
