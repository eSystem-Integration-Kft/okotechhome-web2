Documents

The manufacturer offers a data sheet and a declaration of performance for each model. These have to be gathered in Hungarian, with versions.

What this page will be

A single versioned document centre

The aim is for the manufacturer's documents and those needed for project preparation, covering the EPURECO models ÖkoTech sells, to be available in one place and up to date.

The manufacturer offers a data sheet and a declaration of performance for each model, along with shared installation and operating documentation for the range. These have to be gathered in Hungarian, with versions.

The contents of the document library

What belongs here

Beside every file: model, document type, manufacturer, version or date of issue, and „current / archived” status.

What has to be handled separately

The regulatory content

Legal and permitting information does not belong in a static PDF. A good example is the legislative summary still on our site, last updated in 2012: it has stood there for more than a decade while the regulations it cites have been amended several times since.

So legal content has to be maintained in a separate, regularly updated system, not among the product documents. The old summary should be archived — not deleted, but clearly dated and marked „archived”.

In the meantime

Ask for what you need

Until the document library is assembled, we send the papers you need directly. Write and tell us which model and which document, and what you need it for.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Are the documents available in Hungarian too?

Some of the manufacturer's documents are available in Hungarian as well. We are currently establishing exactly which — ask about the specific model and we will send what is available.

Where do I find documentation for my older unit?

Give us the type and roughly the year of installation — we will find the documentation from that time. An older EPURECO does not necessarily belong with today's data sheet.
