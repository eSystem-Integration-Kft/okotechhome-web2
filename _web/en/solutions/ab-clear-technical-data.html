Technical data

The single official web source of technical data for the range — so that the same figure does not appear elsewhere in different versions.

A single source

Why this page is needed

This page is the A.B.Clear range's single official web source of technical data. The aim is that the same figure should not appear in different versions across the FAQs, the technical articles and the permit examples.

Where a figure has not yet been reconciled, we say so here — we neither pass over it in silence nor pick arbitrarily between the versions.

Outlet water characteristics

The measured values and what lies behind them

Our current technical material gives the following characteristic values for the outlet water, citing a VITUKI test: 55 mg/l COD(Cr), 15 mg/l BOD5, 18 mg/l suspended solids, 9 mg/l ammonium nitrogen, 20 mg/l total nitrogen, 5 mg/l total phosphorus.

These are usable, strong figures — but they can only be read with the context that belongs to them: on which model, under what test conditions, when they were measured, and what limits of application they hold under. This has to be tied to the full test report.

The water quality values are not product properties in the sense of being guaranteed in all circumstances: the actual outlet water quality depends on the load, the operation and the condition of the biology too.

On regulatory conformity

Not a timeless product property

Our current technical content carries a claim of the „conforms to Decree 28/2004” kind. A conformity claim of that sort always has to be tied to the the current text of the decree and it cannot be treated as though it were a permanent property of the product.

Regulations and standards are amended from time to time. So every conformity reference on this page has to state which version it applies to and when we last checked it.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

What quality of water does the unit produce?

On the measurements available, the outlet water is substantially treated in terms of COD, BOD5, suspended solids and nutrient content. We publish the specific values above with their source — but these are test results, not values guaranteed in all circumstances.

Does it conform to the standard?

The units carry the CE marking on the basis of conformity to EN 12566-3, established by accredited testing. We are working on making the documents directly available, so that this is not a claim but a verifiable paper.

Is the treated water drinkable?

No, and it is not made for that. It is unfit for human consumption in any circumstances. Disposing of the treated water is a design task in its own right.
