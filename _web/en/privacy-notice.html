New service

AI-based quote comparison

MISSING DATA — to be supplied before go-live: the exact name and registered office of the AI provider, the contractual safeguards (a processor agreement, and standard contractual clauses for any transfer outside the EU), and whether the provider uses submitted data for training. Without these this section is incomplete.

On the website elérhető ajánlat-összehasonlító modul a feltöltött dokumentumokat transfers it to an artificial intelligence provider for processing. We single this out because it is the only function on this site that sends your document to a third party.

How it works

  1. You upload one or more quotes from your own device.
  2. We extract text from the document on our own server.
  3. We send the extracted text to the AI provider for analysis.
  4. We display the result of the analysis to you in the browser.
  5. We delete the uploaded file and the extracted text after processing. Neither is stored with us or written to a database.

What to watch for

Quotes may contain personal data — a name, an address, a telephone number. If you would rather not send these, redact them in the document before uploading. The module does not need personal data for the comparison.

By uploading, you expressly consent to us transferring the content of the document to an AI provider for the purpose of analysis. The only consequence of refusing consent is that the module cannot be used — every other function of the site remains available.

Your rights

What you can ask of us

You may exercise the following rights at any time. You can submit your request at kapcsolat@okotechhome.hu or by post to our registered office.

  • Access. You may ask whether we process data about you and, if so, what data, for what purpose, for how long, and to whom we pass it. You may also request a copy.
  • Rectification. You may ask for inaccurate data to be corrected and incomplete data to be completed.
  • Erasure. You may ask for your data to be erased if the purpose has ceased, if you withdraw your consent, or if the processing is unlawful. Erasure cannot be enforced where legislation requires retention — for accounting records, for instance.
  • Restriction of processing. You may ask us only to store your data and not process it further — while you contest its accuracy, for instance.
  • Data portability. You may request data processed automatically on the basis of consent or a contract in a structured, machine-readable format, and ask for it to be transmitted to another controller.
  • Objection. You may object to processing based on legitimate interest. We then continue only if we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.
  • Withdrawal of consent. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

Time limit for replying

We reply to a request without undue delay and in any event within one month . That period may be extended by two months where the request is complex; we inform you of any extension within one month of receiving the request.

Automated decision-making

We take no decision about you based solely on automated processing that produces legal effects, and we do not carry out profiling. The output of the quote comparison and pre-screening modules is information, not a decision — the actual technical and commercial decisions are taken by people.

Remedies

If you disagree with how we process your data

Come to us first — most questions are resolved fastest that way. If that does not succeed, you have the following options.

Judicial remedy

You may bring proceedings against the controller or the processor before a court. At your choice, the action may also be brought before the regional court for your domicile or residence. The jurisdiction and contact details of the regional courts are at birosag.hu.

Personal data breach

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights, we inform you without undue delay. We notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Do I have to give my data?

No. Giving it is voluntary — but without certain data we cannot reply. For a quote, for instance, we need contact details and the project's basic data. On the forms we mark which fields are required, and why.

Do you send a newsletter?

A reply to your enquiry is not a newsletter. We send marketing communications only with separate, express consent, and every such message carries a simple way to unsubscribe.

What happens to the quotes I upload?

We extract text from the document, send it to the AI provider for analysis, then display the result. The file and the extracted text are deleted after processing — neither is stored with us. If the document contains personal data, you can redact it before uploading.

How long do you keep my data?

It varies by processing operation — the table above gives it for each row. The longest is the 8-year retention of accounting records required by law; we cannot shorten that even on request.
