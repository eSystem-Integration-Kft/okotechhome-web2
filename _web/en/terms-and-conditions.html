The contract

How it comes about, and what it contains

The request for a quote, and the quote

  1. The Customer requests a quote — through the site's form, by email, by phone or in person.
  2. The Seller sends the quote within 48 hours of receiving the request, on working days.
  3. The Customer may accept the quote within 48 hours of it being sent; after that the Seller is no longer bound by it.
  4. The contract comes about when the acceptance of the quote reaches the Seller.

The Seller's obligations

  • transferring title to and handing over the product in the quantity, quality and description set out in the contract
  • carrying out the installation where separately ordered
  • conducting the technical handover procedure
  • demonstrating the operation of the unit and handing over the user guide

The Customer's obligations

  • paying the purchase price before delivery or on handover
  • examining the product on receipt
  • providing the electricity needed for the work
  • ensuring access to the work area
  • arranging for construction debris to be removed, unless the contract provides otherwise

Performance

Delivery, telepítés, átadás

Delivery

The product is delivered within the deadline stated in the confirmation; the deadline runs from the day after the full purchase price is received. We can undertake delivery at a specific hour only by separate agreement.

Receipt and examination

On receipt the Customer must examine the contents of the consignment and confirm receipt. Damage arising from carriage must be reported on receipt, to the carrier; the Seller is not liable for such damage.

Installation és műszaki átadás

The Seller carries out the installation safely, competently, economically and on time. Weather may delay performance. The Seller acts on the Customer's instructions but must warn where an instruction is inexpedient or unlawful. Performance is evidenced by the documented technical handover; if the Customer does not attend the handover, taking actual possession produces the legal effects of performance.

Storage

Where delivery takes place more than one month after payment of the full purchase price, the Seller stores the product free of charge until the agreed delivery date. For the period after that, a storage fee of HUF 3,000 + VAT per day may be charged.

Arranging and cancelling appointments

  • the Seller confirms the agreed time by email; without the Customer's confirmation the slot is not reserved
  • the first change of appointment is free of charge
  • for a repeated cancellation or change, an administration fee of HUF 15,000 + VAT may be charged
  • cancellation is free of charge until 16:00 on the second working day before the service date; after that the call-out fee may be charged

Payment

Payment terms

The Customer pays the purchase price after confirmation, by bank transfer or card. The Seller sends the invoice electronically to the email address given; the Customer accepts electronic invoicing unless they state otherwise.

In the event of late payment the Seller may charge default interest under the Civil Code from the day the payment falls into arrears. Collection costs are borne by the Customer. Where a debt is overdue, the Seller may suspend further service until it is settled.

Consumer rights

Right of withdrawal for distance contracts

Where the contract is concluded with a consumer at a distance, the consumer has a right of withdrawal within 14 days without giving reasons under Government Decree 45/2014 (II. 26.).

The time limit

The 14 days run from the day on which the consumer, or a third party other than the carrier indicated by them, takes receipt of the product. Where there are several items, it runs from the last item received. For a contract for services, from the day the contract is concluded.

How it is exercised

By an unequivocal statement — by post, by email, or using the model form in Annex 2 to Government Decree 45/2014. The time limit is met if the statement is sent before it expires.

Returning the product

The product must be returned without undue delay and in any event within 14 days of notifying the withdrawal. The direct cost of return is borne by the consumer. The consumer is liable for any diminished value resulting from use beyond what is necessary to establish the nature and characteristics of the product.

Refund

The Seller refunds the sums paid by the consumer, including delivery costs, within 14 days of becoming aware of the withdrawal — except any additional cost arising from choosing a delivery method other than the least expensive standard one. The refund is made using the original means of payment. The Seller may withhold the refund until it has received the product back, or until the consumer has proved that it was returned.

When it cannot be exercised

  • after the service has been performed in full, where the consumer expressly consented in advance to performance beginning and acknowledged the loss of the right of withdrawal
  • for products not prefabricated, made on the consumer's instructions or at their express request, or clearly personalised to the consumer
  • for urgent repair or maintenance work carried out at the consumer's express request
  • in the further cases specified by law

For a contract for services, where cancellation occurs after 14 days, 30% of the fee under the order may be charged as a cancellation penalty.

Defective performance

Warranty for defects, product warranty, guarantee

Warranty for defects

In the event of defective performance the Customer may request repair or replacement, unless that is impossible or would involve disproportionate additional cost. Where that is not possible or the Seller has not undertaken it, the Customer may claim a proportionate price reduction, may have the defect remedied at the Seller's expense, or — as a last resort — may withdraw from the contract. Withdrawal is not available for an insignificant defect.

The defect must be notified within two months of discovery. The claim becomes time-barred two years after performance — one year for a business. Within six months of performance no condition applies beyond notifying the defect; after six months the Customer must prove that the defect already existed at the time of performance.

Product warranty

Where movable property is defective, the consumer may request repair or replacement from the manufacturer or distributor. A product is defective if it does not meet the quality requirements in force when it was placed on the market, or does not have the characteristics stated by the manufacturer. The claim may be brought within two years of it being placed on the market. A warranty-for-defects claim and a product warranty claim cannot be brought simultaneously for the same defect.

Guarantee

For a consumer contract the Seller provides a one-year guarantee on the unit from handover or installation. The guarantee covers the tank structure and the built-in units — the blower, the distributors, the pipework — provided the Seller carried out both delivery and installation.

The guarantee does not cover

  • natural wear under intended use
  • a defect arising from improper use, failure to maintain, or unauthorised modification
  • a defect arising from installation not carried out by the Seller
  • damage occurring during carriage where the Seller did not carry it out
  • the performance of the bacterial culture — its condition is materially affected by the chemicals and substances put into the system, which is under the Customer's control

Enforcing the guarantee is conditional on documented proof of intended use and maintenance. Where the Customer cannot evidence the maintenance, that may limit or exclude the guarantee claim.

Liability

Limits of liability

The Seller limits its liability for defective performance to 50% of the net fee paid to it in the course of performance. This limitation does not extend to breaches caused intentionally, or to those harming human life, bodily integrity or health — the limitation cannot apply to these by force of law either.

The Seller is not liable for consequential damage — such as lost profit or harm to reputation — and is released where the failure to perform results from an unforeseeable circumstance outside its control (force majeure).

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Is this website an online shop?

No. You can request a quote here, but you cannot buy directly. The contract comes about separately, on acceptance of the quote — which is why these terms concern the sale and service contract above all.

How long is your quote valid?

For 48 hours from when it is sent. That may seem short, but the prices and capacities used to build the quote can be held for that long. If you need more time, say so — we can usually extend it.

How long is the guarantee?

For a consumer contract, one year from handover or installation. A warranty-for-defects claim can be brought for two years regardless. The guarantee is conditional on documented, intended use and maintenance.

Does the guarantee cover the bacterial culture?

No. The condition of the bacterial culture is materially affected by what goes into the system — which, in use, is under the Customer's control. The guarantee therefore does not cover it. The operation page sets out in detail what must not be put into the system.
