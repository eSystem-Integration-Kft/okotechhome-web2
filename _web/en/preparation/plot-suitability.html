Plot suitability

The tank will almost always fit somewhere. The system only works, though, if the treated water also has somewhere to go — and that is the harder question. Here we go through what decides it.

What this section decides

Plot suitability is not a single figure

The plot suitability of a wastewater system is not the answer to a single question. The soil, the groundwater, the available area, the level of the wastewater pipe leaving the house, the terrain, wells and other sensitive objects, and the possibility of disposing of the treated water together determine what can be built on the plot — and in what arrangement.

Government Decree 147/2010 itself requires septic tank and individual biological systems to be sized taking account of the soil's conditions, the depth of the subsurface water and the volume of wastewater. Further conditions apply in areas with high groundwater and in highly sensitive areas. Knowing a single figure is therefore not enough.

Ez a szakasz nem terméket ajánl. A célja az, hogy végigvezesse a kritikus telekfeltételeken, megmutassa, melyik adatot honnan szerezheti meg, és a végén látható legyen, hol tart: elegendő-e a meglévő adat az előszűréshez, or mérés, illetve szakértői vizsgálat szükséges.

Two separate decisions

The tank will fit — the water also needs somewhere to go

Whether the tank can be installed

  • The physical placement of the unit on the plot
  • Enough room for the tank and the mechanical plant
  • The relationship between the wastewater pipe level and the receiving level
  • A separate structural solution where there is groundwater — anchoring, a concrete basin
  • Machine access and lifting the tank in
  • Later access for servicing

Disposal of the treated water

  • Where the volume leaving each day goes
  • The soil's MEASURED capacity to accept water, not its name
  • The highest seasonal groundwater level
  • The space the soakaway needs, as a function of the load
  • Wells, water base protection, area sensitivity classification
  • Where infiltration into the ground is unsuitable: another route for disposal

Why this distinction matters

The second question is the harder one

The most common misunderstanding is to treat the two questions as one. That the tank can be anchored safely even in higher groundwater — ÖkoTech uses a concrete basin around the plastic tank in such cases — does not mean that infiltrating the treated water into the ground is also appropriate on the same plot.

Water disposal is itself two questions: technically possible (will the soil accept the daily volume), and whether it is permissible in law and from an environmental standpoint alkalmazható-e az adott helyen. Bármelyik megállíthatja or módosíthatja a projektet.

The practical consequence: if the route for water disposal is unsettled, the project is not ready for a quote — even where the tank's position is already fixed.

The factors to examine

What determines a plot's suitability?

Seven topics, each on its own page. The order is not compulsory, but it has a logic: soil and groundwater affect most of what follows, while access is what emerges last if it is not looked at in time.

  • Soil and infiltration capacity

    The name of the soil — sandy, clay — is not a sizing figure in itself. The question is how quickly the soil on site can accept water.

    Soil and infiltration→

  • Groundwater

    It is not today's water level that counts but the highest that occurs seasonally. Anchoring the tank and infiltration are two separate questions.

    Groundwater→

  • Wells and protective distance

    From a water protection standpoint, the sealed tank and the point where water actually enters the ground are not the same object.

    Wells and protection→

  • Plot size and available area

    The question is not the plot's square metres but the area genuinely usable once the house, the well, the driveway and the utilities are accounted for.

    Plot size→

  • Gradient and pipe depth

    The height of the pipe leaving the house determines how deep the unit's inlet can sit — and whether a pumping station is needed.

    Gradient and pipe depth→

  • Vehicle loading and access

    Vehicles cannot pass over the tank without a separate arrangement, and the system must remain accessible later.

    Loading and access→

  • How do I gather the data?

    Source by source: where it can be obtained, who can supply it, and which of them an estimate will not do for.

    Gathering data→

Four possible states

Nem „alkalmas" or „nem alkalmas"

Plot suitability is rarely binary. At the end of the examination the plot falls into one of these — and each carries a different next step.

  • 01

    Standard. On the data known there is no identified critical obstacle, and the usual arrangement is likely. This is neither a permit nor a final sizing — the design can proceed in the usual way.

  • 02

    Conditional. A telepítés megvalósítható, de külön műszaki elem szükséges: tartályrögzítés, magasítás, átemelő, betonakna or nagyobb szikkasztó. Ezek a projekt költségét és kivitelezési idejét is befolyásolják.

  • 03

    To be examined. Egy or több kritikus adat hiányzik, or csak becslés áll rendelkezésre. Itt nem a telek a probléma, hanem az adat: mérés, dokumentum or helyszíni felmérés zárja le.

  • 04

    Not currently evidenced. Az ismert körülmények alapján a tervezett megoldás nem támasztható alá — jellemzően vízvédelmi or talajvíz-okból. Ilyenkor más vízelhelyezési irány, más technológia or szakértői vizsgálat következik.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Elég, ha megadom a telek adatait, or ki kell jönniük?

Attól függ, melyik adatot ismeri és milyen minőségben. Ha a kritikus adatok — talaj, talajvíz, szabad terület, csőkilépési szint — dokumentumból or mérésből ismertek, sok esetben elegendő a távoli előszűrés. Ha ezek egy része csak becslés, a felmérés nem formalitás, hanem az egyetlen mód a lezárásukra. Ezt minden témakörnél külön jelezzük.

What if I simply do not know one of the figures?

A „nem tudom” érvényes válasz, és nem jelent kizárást. Adatbeszerzési útvonal következik belőle: megmondjuk, honnan szerezhető meg, ki tudja megadni, és hogy elegendő-e hozzá dokumentum, or mérés kell.

Is a neighbour's experience acceptable as data?

Előzetes jelzésnek hasznos — különösen a szezonális talajvízről —, de nem azonos értékű egy mérési eredménnyel or hivatalos dokumentummal. Az előszűrésben külön jelöljük, hogy egy adat becsült, dokumentált or mért, mert a továbblépés ettől függ.

Does high groundwater rule the system out from the start?

No. It breaks into two separate questions: installing the tank safely, and whether the treated water can be disposed of. There is often a technical solution to the first. The second, though, depends not only on the groundwater but on water protection and area classification, and may require a separate examination.

Kap-e engedélyt or hivatalos igazolást az előszűrésből?

No. Pre-screening is technical orientation: it shows what looks like an obstacle and what data is missing. An authority rules on whether a permit can be obtained, a qualified designer on sizing, and a specialist on hydrogeological questions.
