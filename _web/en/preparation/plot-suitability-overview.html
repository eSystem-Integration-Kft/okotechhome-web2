Plot suitability overview

This is not a detailed guide but a map: what has to be examined, in what order, and where it leads next. The detail lives on the individual pages.

What this page is for

A map, not a detailed guide

This page runs briefly through the questions that have to be answered before the siting of a system and a direction for water disposal can be recommended responsibly. Under each topic it says only why it matters, what you need to know about it, és where you can go next. The detail lives on the individual pages.

The order is not accidental. The first question is the mains sewer position and the plot's territorial classification, because a legal or water-protection circumstance can affect whether a standalone system is in play at all, before any technical detail.

The test sorrendje

What is worth settling first?

If your time is limited, work through them in this order: the earlier points determine what the later ones even mean.

  • 01

    Mains sewer and the legal starting position. Whether a mains sewer is available, and what the municipality's sensitivity classification is. If the plot lies in a designated water base protection zone, that affects everything else.

  • 02

    Soil and actual infiltration capacity. Not the name of the soil but the measured capacity to take water. This determines whether the treated water can be disposed of on site, and over how large an area.

  • 03

    Groundwater. The highest water level that occurs seasonally. It bears on installing the tank and on infiltration separately.

  • 04

    Wells and other water protection features. The position, type and use of your own and neighbouring wells. A sealed holding tank and a soakaway are judged separately here.

  • 05

    Genuinely free area. What remains once the house, outbuildings, driveway, utilities and trees are accounted for — and what will not be built on later either.

  • 06

    Pipe exit level and ground slope. The level of the pipe from the house determines the unit's inlet level; the slope determines whether the water can be conveyed by gravity.

  • 07

    Vehicle loading and access. Access for the installation, and lasting access for servicing. This is what emerges latest if it is not looked at in good time.

Data quality

Which figures may be estimated, and which have to be measured?

Typically sufficient from a document or the owner's knowledge

  • Address, land registry number, plot size — title deed, map extract
  • Mains sewer position — water utility, local authority
  • The position of the house and the free area — site plan
  • Direction of the ground slope — site walk, photo
  • Position and use of the well — site walk
  • Access width, route for machinery — on-site measurement, photo
  • Future garden and building plans — you know these

This requires measurement or a documented investigation

  • The actual infiltration capacity of the soil — percolation test
  • Geotechnical stratification — expert report
  • The seasonally highest groundwater level — measurement or an expert report
  • The exact invert depth of the pipe exit — drawings or levelling
  • Water base protection classification — official source
  • Depth of the well and its aquifer conditions — well documentation
  • Whether infiltration is permitted in a highly sensitive area — the authority

What changes the design, and what stops it

Not every problem carries the same weight

Some plot conditions only affect the design changes it: a structural solution for higher groundwater, a pumping station for a deep pipe exit, a concrete chamber for vehicle loading, a larger infiltration area for poorer percolation. These mean cost and construction time, not exclusion.

Others, however, a separate expert requires it: an uncertain or designated water base protection situation, a highly sensitive area, unknown stratification, a plan to discharge to a surface receiving water. In these it is not the manufacturer of the unit that decides but a hydrogeologist, a qualified designer or the authority.

That separation is exactly what this overview is for: before anyone quotes, it should be visible which category the plot falls into.

Next step

Start with the figure that determines the most

The actual infiltration capacity of the soil and the seasonal groundwater level are the two figures that determine most of the others. If you can obtain only one, start with these.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

How far do these figures get me before I request a quote?

The pre-screener tells you whether there is a visible technical or water-protection risk and what is still missing. It gives no price, no final model and no statement that a permit can be obtained — those need a person and more data.

What is the sensitivity classification?

The sensitivity classification of municipalities with regard to subsurface water appears in an official register, and it affects the conditions under which treated wastewater may be disposed of into the geological medium. The classification changes from time to time, so it always has to be checked from a current source.
