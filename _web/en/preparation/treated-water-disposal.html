Treated water disposal

Choosing the unit gets you halfway. The volume leaving each day does not vanish: it has to be disposed of somewhere — and that is the harder question.

Why this is a topic of its own

Choosing the unit does not close the project

Choosing the biological treatment unit gets you halfway. The other half is where the treated water leaving the unit goes, and by what means — and this has to be settled at the design stage, not in the week of installation.

The volume leaving each day is roughly the volume of wastewater arriving. It does not vanish: it has to be disposed of somewhere. The route for disposal is therefore a precondition of quoting, not an afterthought — where it is unsettled, the project is not ready for a quote.

This section goes through the routes for disposal and the conditions attaching to each. It offers no universal recommendation: the outcome is a likely route for disposal, a hozzá szükséges telekadatokkal és az esetleg szükséges vizsgálatokkal together.

Routes for disposal

Where can the treated water go?

Three main routes, with different technical and regulatory conditions. They are not equivalent alternatives: the first is the most common, the third a separate technical and legal branch.

  • Soakaway infiltration

    The most common on-site solution: disposing of the already treated water below ground, in the soil. It turns on the soil's measured capacity to accept water, on the groundwater and on the free area.

    Soakaway infiltration→

  • Drainage field

    With a septic tank system the field formed in the soil is not an accessory but part of the treatment. Its space requirement is therefore larger, and it cannot be moved freely.

    Drainage field→

  • Reed bed disposal

    Use within the plot on a planted area. With an exact technical definition and clear limits on use — it is not the same as regulated agricultural irrigation.

    Reed bed disposal→

The most common misunderstanding

Soakaway infiltration or tisztítómező?

After an active biological system — infiltration

  • Treatment has taken place IN THE UNIT
  • At the soakaway's a kilépő víz elhelyezésére szolgál
  • The space requirement is typically smaller
  • Sizing is a function of the daily volume and the measured infiltration
  • The position can be chosen, with due care

After a septic tank — a drainage field

  • ANAEROBIC pre-treatment takes place in the tank
  • A significant part of the aerobic process takes place IN THE FIELD
  • The field is part of the technology, not a discharge
  • The space requirement is therefore typically larger
  • The legislation itself names a facility “with a drainage field”

What it depends on

What decides which route can be considered?

Ezek together hatnak. Egyetlen kedvezőtlen tényező is átírhatja az irányt, de egyetlen kedvező sem elég önmagában.

  • 01

    The soil's measured infiltration capacity. Not the soil's name. This determines whether the soil will accept the daily volume, and over what area.

  • 02

    The groundwater's seasonal maximum. Nem a mai vízállás. At the soakaway's és a talajvíz viszonya a működés alapfeltétele.

  • 03

    The daily volume leaving the unit. It follows from the load, and it directly determines the soakaway's size.

  • 04

    The free area available. The area required also has to fit — after the house, the well, the driveway and the structures planned.

  • 05

    Wells and water base protection. From a water protection standpoint, the point at which treated water enters the ground is an object in its own right.

  • 06

    Area sensitivity classification. In a highly sensitive area, separate conditions apply to discharge into the geological medium.

What this section does not do

There is no universal recommendation for disposal

The route for disposal is plot-specific. The same unit may need a different solution on two neighbouring plots — and in some places on-site disposal cannot be achieved at all. What we give here is therefore not a recommendation but conditions and a decision path.

A Discharge to a surface watercourse is therefore a separate technical and legal branch: it requires a water-rights permit and compliance with discharge conditions, and cannot be treated as a simple alternative to on-site disposal. Where it arises, a qualified designer and consultation with the authority follow.

Nor do we state a specific price. The cost level of the different arrangements differs — a larger soakaway, pumped conveyance, a special arrangement all bear on cost — but that can only be read in the context of the project as a whole, not on its own.

Next step

Start with the most common route

On most family home projects, infiltration is the first route to examine. Where the soil and the groundwater permit it, it is the simplest solution — and it becomes clear at once what has to be measured for it.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

How much water leaves the unit each day?

Roughly as much as the wastewater arriving — biological treatment breaks down the pollutant load, it does not make the water disappear. The soakaway therefore cannot be sized to the space left in the yard: it has to be sized to the daily volume.

If the soil is good, is water disposal settled?

Nem feltétlenül. Jó vízbefogadó talaj mellett is akadály lehet a magas szezonális talajvíz, a szűk szabad terület, a kút közelsége or a terület érzékenységi besorolása. A talaj az egyik feltétel, nem az egyetlen.

Bevezethető a víz a patakba or az árokba?

This is a separate technical and legal question: it requires a water-rights permit and compliance with discharge requirements, and cannot be considered at every site. It is not a simple alternative to on-site infiltration — it has to be discussed with a qualified designer and with the authority.

Can I water the garden with the treated water?

Use within the plot carries technical and usage conditions, and not every use is permissible. We treat this on a separate page, because the question is a sensitive one: ornamental planting is one thing and food crops another, and regulated agricultural reuse is a separate legal category again.

When is an expert needed?

Ha a talajvíz nem ismert, a szivárgóképesség nincs mérve, a telek kijelölt vízbázisvédelmi területet érinthet, felszíni befogadó merül fel, or nagyobb illetve nem kommunális projektről van szó. Ezeket külön oldal veszi végig.
