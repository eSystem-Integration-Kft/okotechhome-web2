Load and capacity

The capacity required does not follow from the number of people. Water volume, organic load and the distribution of use over time decide it together — occupancy is only the opening question.

The opening question

“How many people?” is only the first question

The capacity a treatment unit needs does not follow simply from the number of people using the property. The volume of water, a organic loadproduced, and the distribution of use over time és a rövid or tartós peaks determine it together.

Családi háznál a létszám jó közelítés lehet, és nyugodtan azzal érdemes kezdeni. Panziónál, iskolánál, étteremnél or üzemnél viszont ugyanannyi „fő” egészen más vízmennyiséget és szennyezőanyag-terhelést jelenthet — ott a létszám önmagában félrevezető.

This section therefore offers no “Y unit for X people” table but leads to a load profile: the average, the peak, the peak's duration and frequency, and the uncertainties. From that the capacity range can be set on a sound basis.

Two separate loads

What has to be measured separately and sized separately

Hydraulic load — how much WATER arrives

  • Napi átlagos volume of water, m³/nap or liter/nap
  • A peak arriving in a short time — morning showers, the washing machine
  • A vízszámlából or mérőóráról leolvasható
  • On a new build it has to be estimated
  • This determines the unit's throughput capacity
  • And it also gives the volume to be DISPOSED OF each day

Organic load — how much MATTER has to be broken down

  • The quantity of biologically degradable pollutant
  • Its unit is the population equivalent (PE): 1 PE = 60 g BOD5 per day
  • It cannot be read off the water meter
  • It can be measured by laboratory analysis
  • This determines the size of the biological stage
  • High water use does not mean a proportionately high organic load

What to avoid

Four typical sizing mistakes

All of them come from the same place: sizing from a single number.

  • 01

    Sizing to the maximum. If a few large family gatherings a year set the unit's size, the system is persistently underloaded on every other day of the year — and that is an operating problem in its own right.

  • 02

    Ignoring the regular peak. Twice a year is not the same as guests arriving every weekend. Frequency and duration count at least as much as the size of the peak.

  • 03

    Confusing water volume with organic load. High water use does not mean a proportionately high pollutant load — nor the reverse. In a restaurant it is precisely the latter that is the bottleneck.

  • 04

    Leaving out future expansion. Ha tudható, hogy two years bővül a család or a szálláshely, azt a tervezéskor kell figyelembe venni. Utólag berendezést cserélni lényegesen drágább.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often

Is it not enough to say how many of us live in the house?

Családi háznál ez jó kiindulás, és sokszor elegendő is az első irányhoz. Pontosabb lesz viszont, ha a tényleges vízfogyasztást is megnézzük a vízszámláról, és megmondja, van-e rendszeres vendégcsúcs or tervezett létszámváltozás.

Why do we not size to the maximum occupancy?

Because the bacterial community in an active biological system needs an adequate nutrient load. A persistently underloaded system is an operating problem in its own right — not a “safety margin”. The right approach is the average together with the realistically regular peak.

What is BOD5?

Biochemical oxygen demand over five days: the amount of oxygen the microorganisms in the wastewater use in five days to break down the organic matter. It is the measure of organic load — and the definition of the population equivalent rests on it.

What changes above 50 people?

The individual wastewater treatment category under Government Decree 147/2010 extends to a load of 1–50 PE. Above that the project follows a different regulatory and permitting route, and the design becomes an engineering task.
